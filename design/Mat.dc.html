The museum mat

The work is hung, not fitted: never cropped, never rounded, never stretched past its source resolution. Only the painting and the mat are drawn — no title, no logo, no text, ever.

Visible frame. Lay out inside NSScreen.visibleFrame minus the menu bar; the Dock is ignored when it auto-hides.

Size. The work’s height is a share of the visible height — Small 84%, Medium 76%, Large 66%. Wide works are also capped at 80% of the screen width, whichever limit hits first. If the source image is too small, the margin grows rather than the image upscaling.

Hang height. Centred horizontally; vertically the bottom gap is 1.15× the top gap, so the work sits slightly above centre, the way a curator hangs to eye line.

Shadow. Two soft layers, as if hung a centimetre off the wall: 0 1 2 / 24% and 0 12 32 / 20% black, scaled with the screen’s point size. No frame, no border, no rounding.

Mat colour — Auto. Sample a 4% band around the painting’s edges and average it in OKLCH. Keep the hue, cut chroma to 35% (max 0.035). If the painting is dark (mean L < 0.45) the mat goes deep, L 0.24–0.30; otherwise light, L 0.88–0.92. The result always sits a clear step away from the painting’s edge.

Fixed neutral. #2B2825 in dark appearance, #E9E4DA in light. Custom uses the chosen colour as is.

Changing. The new wallpaper crossfades over 1.2 s once it is fully rendered. Per-display mode renders each screen separately at its own size.
