Vincent van GoghDutch, 1853–1890

Wheat Field with Cypresses, 1889

Oil on canvas · 73.2 × 93.4 cm
The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Painted in the summer of 1889, while Van Gogh was staying at the asylum in Saint-Rémy. He wrote that the cypresses were as beautiful in line and proportion as an Egyptian obelisk, and here they rise like a dark flame against a sky of rolling white clouds.
