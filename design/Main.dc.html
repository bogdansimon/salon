Johannes Vermeer Dutch, 1632–1675

The Milkmaid, c. 1660

Oil on canvas · 45.5 × 41 cm
Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

A kitchen maid pours milk into an earthenware bowl, her attention entirely on the thin white stream. Vermeer builds the quiet of the scene out of light: it falls from the window at left across the bare wall, the bread and the brass. Small points of thick paint make the crust glisten. Technical research has shown that he painted out objects that once stood behind her, leaving the plaster that gives the picture its stillness.
