Accent rule. Take the painting’s most common mid-tone (k-means, k = 5, skip near-greys), then fix it in OKLCH: light L 0.45, dark L 0.78, chroma clamped to 0.08, hue kept. Soft variant = accent at 10% (light) / 14% (dark). Animate changes with the artwork crossfade.
